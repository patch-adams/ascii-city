The shelf

drag to arrange · it remembers

Ten books on three planks. Pull one and it tells you what it is; the second box is yours, and whatever you write in it stays on this device.

You can drag the spines into any order, and drag the objects anywhere along the shelf. Where a thing sits is saved. That's the whole idea — a list is nothing to remember, a place is.
